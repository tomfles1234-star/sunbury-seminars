About Sunbury Seminars

Sunbury Seminars, Inc., is a proud sponsor of Dental Continuing Education in the state of Ohio. We focus primarily in the areas of pain and anxiety control (minimal to moderate sedation and deep/general anesthesia) and pharmacology.

Our mission is to provide the highest quality continuing education to the dental practitioner. Our courses present contemporary information which has relevance and clinical practicality for the dentist and is taught by experts in their field who practice what they teach on a daily basis.
